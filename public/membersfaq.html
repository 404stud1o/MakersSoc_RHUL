Member's FAQs


HOW CAN I GET MY DESIGN TO BE FABRICATED?

You can e-mail your design to makers@royalholloway.su, and tell us what colour, and material (for laser-cut only) you would like.
The files should be either an STL for 3D printing, or an SVG for laser-cutting.

Colour Options
Laser Cutting
SOLID: Black, White, Purple, Blue, Green, Red
TRANSPARENT: Clear, Light Blue, Dark Green, Red
FLUORESCENT: Orange, Yellow, Dark Blue, Red

3D Printing
PLA: Black, White, Red, Orange, Green, Blue...

SOLDERING CERTIFICATION

A soldering certification session is required for all members to use the soldering irons in our electronic laboratories.
This is to ensure that all members are able to use the equipment safely and effectively.
Please request for a session with the form below if you are not yet certified with us, thanks.

WHEN CAN I ACCESS THE ELETRONICS & PC LABS?

As a member, you can access the SHILLING-1-06 electronics lab as long as there is at least a technician & a member of the committee present.
The preset lab access hours for societies are Wednesday 13:00-17:00, which will usually be our workshop times.
If you would like use the lab equipments outside these hours, please ask one of the committee members for the availability of the lab.
Alternatively, you can also check the detailed timetable of the lab on the RHUL Timetable System. Simply go to 'Location', select the 'Electronics Engineering' department, and 'SHILLING-1-06', then select the week(s) and "View Timetable"

*** There will be no access available during week 12 & 28 due to EE1000 & EE2000 project deadlines. ***


WORKSHOPS

All workshops will be scheduled on a Wednesday afternoon, usually 15:00-17:00, at SHILLING-1-06 electronic lab.
The detailed schedule for all the workshops will usually be published on weekly basis, please keep track of any news & announcements through the member's WhatsApp group chat , our Discord server , the event calendar on the website or our Instagram .
